GS Location Changer - User Guide

Complete Feature Walkthrough & Tips

⚡ Quick 3-Step Start

  1. Select Location & Language: In the Country & Lang tab, click the search box to pick your target country (e.g. United States) and language (e.g. English), then click Apply Location & Language.
  2. Search on Google: Type your search directly in Chrome's URL bar, or type in the Quick Search bar at the bottom of this popup and press Enter.
  3. See Localized Results: Google automatically loads localized search results for that exact country and language!

🌐 1. Country & Language Switching

Google uses two primary URL parameters to control your results:

  • gl (Country / Geolocation): Directs Google to use that specific country's search index (e.g. gl=us for USA, gl=gb for UK, gl=pk for Pakistan, gl=de for Germany).
  • hl (Host Language): Sets the language of Google's search interface, buttons, menus, and spell-check suggestions.
  • lr (Language Restrict): When enabled in Settings, strictly limits results to web pages written in that specific language.
💡 Tip: Click the search input box to browse all 166 countries or 79 languages with full arrow key navigation!

📍 2. Local SEO & Precise GPS Pinpoint

Ideal for local businesses, SEO agencies, and geo-targeted ranking checks:

  • Preset Local SEO Cities: Select from global hubs (New York, London, Dubai, Tokyo, etc.) to instantly load exact coordinates and Google canonical names.
  • Latitude & Longitude (-90 to 90 lat, -180 to 180 lng): Enter precise GPS coordinates. When you search "coffee shop near me" or click Google's "Use precise location", our extension feeds these exact coordinates to Google's HTML5 Geolocation API.
  • Canonical UULE Generator: Google's official secret weapon for local search URLs. Type any city or area name (e.g. Austin, Texas, United States) and click Generate UULE. This generates a Google Protobuf parameter (w+CAIQICI...) that enforces city-level SERPs.
  • Clear GPS: Click Clear GPS anytime to reset back to general country-level search.

⭐ 3. Favorites & Presets

Save and switch between client configurations in a split second:

  • Configure any location, language, coordinates, and UULE.
  • Click Save to Favorites, give it a recognizable name (e.g. "Client A - London Local"), and save.
  • Open the Favorites tab and click Apply on any saved card to activate it in 1 click!
  • Export / Import: Use the Export button to save your favorites as a JSON backup or import them on other machines safely.

⚙️ 4. Settings & Power Tools

  • Auto-Apply: Automatically synchronizes your chosen location whenever you search from Chrome's top address bar.
  • Non-Personalized Search (pws=0): Strips search history and cookie bias to reveal true, neutral, organic ranking positions.
  • Master Switch (Top Right): Turn all location spoofing ON or OFF with a single click. When OFF, Google uses your standard real location.

🏷️ 5. Toolbar Icon Badge

The extension icon on your browser toolbar shows your active status:

  • Green Badge (e.g., US, GB, PK, DE): Spoofing is active for that country.
  • Grey Badge (OFF): Spoofing is disabled.
